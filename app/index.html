Roadmap to the platinum

One playthrough is enough. There is no mission replay and no difficulty setting, so keep a save at the start and end of each chapter — 15 manual save slots, 1 autosave — and do the Chapter 1 meditation below before you leave Chapter 1. Cheats disable trophies and saving, so leave them off. (PowerPyx)

Stage 1 — Story, 60–80 hours

Play Chapters 1–5 in order. Chapter trophies and the mission trophies pop automatically and cannot be missed. Do the optional questlines as they appear: Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy, Tyrone and Bret all have final-mission trophies. Scan vehicles with WAiNK and study animals with binoculars as you go — that progress already counts.

Missable warning: Positive Mental Attitude is the only possibly-missable trophy. In Chapter 1, after Down to Earth, talk to Lucia in the kitchen in the morning, follow her to the balcony and choose Accept to meditate. It is unconfirmed whether that meditation is required, but PowerPyx earned the trophy after doing it.

Stage 2 — 100% checklist, about 100 hours

After the story, open Progress, Stats, 100% Completion. It is closer to 60% of true completion, and nothing on it is missable. PowerPyx lists the big asks: 100 optional missions, 200 of 277 base vehicles, 100 of 255 animals, 70 of 80 challenges, 300 stunts, 15 of 18 card sets, 15 of 18 meditations, all 8 bank vaults and all 39 gang operations. KeenGamer adds 100 of 125 Cocaine Flamingos and 5 of 7 Kenton treasure sets.

Stage 3 — Cleanup, about 10 hours

Finish the 8 Strangers & Freaks strands, any challenges left, and the grind trophies: 250 stunts, 42 Shooting Range golds, 20 race wins and 14 Top Fun drone-race locations. A Long Road (gold) pops at 100%, then Vice Got a Price! — the platinum — follows.

⚠ Missable trophies: 1 possibly missable — Positive Mental Attitude (meditate at every spot; do the Chapter 1 balcony meditation). Every other trophy can be earned after the story in free-roam. Source: PowerPyx.
